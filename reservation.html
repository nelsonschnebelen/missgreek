Questions

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended for sunset. You can also walk in — the door doesn't check names.

What time are you open?

Every day from 8 AM: breakfast 8–11 AM, lunch 12–3 PM, no-hurry hour 3–5 PM, and dinner 6–10 PM. An easy evening runs from 10 until close. The kitchen winds down at 10. A precise closing time is not published. Happy hour on the menu is Monday to Thursday, 4 to 7 PM, and Friday to Sunday, 3 to 6 PM.

Is there a gluten-free option?

Pita and sandwiches are served with fries. The menu offers a gluten-free collard green wrap for +3.

Where are you?

Oceanfront at the hotel, Miami Beach, FL. A street address is not published.
